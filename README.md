# NY Pizza at Home

Unofficial personal notes on making New York style pizza in a 500°F home oven with a pizza steel and a peel, based on the method taught by Sam, who posts as [Dough Guy](https://doughguy.co).

**Live page:** https://brycesub.github.io/ny-pizza/

This project isn't made by, or affiliated with, Dough Guy. The recipes and method are his, and the page's colors and fonts are modeled on his site. For the source material, see [his site](https://doughguy.co) and [his Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/@itsdoughguy/shorts).

## What's on the page

- A timeline from three days ahead to bake time
- A dough calculator for size, quantity and thickness
- Sauce recipes
- Steps for stretching, topping, launching and baking
- Troubleshooting and links to the source videos

## How the content was put together

The page was compiled with Claude from the code behind Sam's calculator, his video descriptions, and auto-generated transcripts of some of his Shorts. Not all of his videos have been checked.

- Where his sources disagree, the page follows the newest one.
- Yellow-striped boxes and anything labelled "mine" are Claude's suggestions, not Sam's. That includes the bake time at 500°F, which hasn't been tested.

## Making changes

The page is one file, `index.html`, with no build step or dependencies apart from Google Fonts. `apple-touch-icon.png` is its home screen icon. To preview it, open the file in a browser.

GitHub Pages publishes from the root of `main`, so pushing to `main` updates the live page.
